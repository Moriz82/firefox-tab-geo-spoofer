# Privacy Policy

`Tab Geo Spoofer` stores your saved presets in Firefox local extension storage on your device.

## Data We Handle

- **Location information you enter/select** (latitude/longitude and accuracy)
  - Used to spoof geolocation in the current tab.
  - Presets are saved locally in `browser.storage.local`.

## Network Requests

- The popup map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap (`tile.openstreetmap.org`).
- Tile requests include map tile coordinates needed to render the selected map area.

## Data Sharing

- The extension does not send your preset list to our servers.
- The extension does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising code.

## Data Deletion

- Delete presets in the popup UI.
- Uninstalling the extension removes extension storage data.
